# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a positive community:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

This project features real military aircraft. Keep discussion about the game and its aircraft as machines. Please don't use the issue tracker or discussions for political or national arguments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they find inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing the project.

## Reporting

To report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@harrisbasra](https://github.com/harrisbasra), at **harrisbasra@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.
